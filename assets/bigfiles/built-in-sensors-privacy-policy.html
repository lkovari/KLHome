Privacy Policy

App: built-in sensors (com.lkovari.mobile.apps.sensors)
Last updated: 27 July 2026
Publisher: EKLSoft Trade LLC / László Kőváry

Summary

This app lists built-in device sensors and shows live readings on the screen. It does not send sensor data to a remote server, and it does not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

Data we access on the device

  • Body sensors (permission BODY_SENSORS) — for example heart-rate sensors, when present. Values are shown on screen only.
  • Activity recognition (permission ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION) — for step and motion-related sensors, when present. Values are shown on screen only.

These permissions are requested at runtime. Denying them only limits related sensors; the rest of the app remains usable.

Local exports

If you export sensor information, files are written to app-specific storage under Android/data/com.lkovari.mobile.apps.sensors/files/exports/. Exports stay on your device unless you copy or share them yourself.

What we do not do

  • No account or login
  • No transmission of health, activity, or other sensor data to our servers
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with third parties
  • No ads, no third-party analytics in the app

Children

The app is a general utility and is not directed at children.

Contact

For privacy questions about this app, contact the publisher via the Google Play store listing contact details for built-in sensors.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app’s data practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change accordingly.
